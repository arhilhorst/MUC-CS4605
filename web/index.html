At 255 all four tactors are simply held on and behave identically. Below 255 the left-bottom tactor on pin 6 runs at 976 Hz and will whine, because Timer0 cannot be re-prescaled without breaking delay(). Move it to pin 3 before calibrating.

Apparatus

Onset latency is measured host-side, from the moment the play command is written to the moment the board reports its first drive edge. The proposal's gate is under 20 ms.

Stimulus encoding and both angle sets follow Dobbelstein, Henzler & Rukzio, “Unconstrained Pedestrian Navigation based on Vibro-tactile Feedback around the Wristband of a Smartwatch”, CHI EA 2016, pp. 2439–2445. Actuation parameters are ours and are listed in the sketch.
